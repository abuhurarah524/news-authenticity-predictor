BEHIND THE MARGIN

A second look.
Not the last word.

Margin is the reading interface for the News Authenticity Predictor research project. Add a public article link or paste text to prepare a review.

Predictions describe patterns learned from a dataset; they do not verify facts or replace checking original evidence. The experimental model has source and style biases, especially outside political news.

Your article is sent to the app’s server to be processed. Margin does not save article history.
